本機資料
頻道與來源網址、片單訂閱、收藏、最近觀看選擇、暱稱、地區偏好及線路檢查結果保存在裝置內。此版本沒有發行者的雲端帳戶或同步服務。應用設定停用 Android 雲端備份及裝置移轉。

連線與第三方服務
當你匯入／更新片單、載入節目資料、播放或檢查線路時，裝置會向你選取的來源及其轉址／媒體伺服器傳送網絡請求。這些服務可收到 IP 位址、請求時間、裝置／播放器的請求資訊，以及網址內的參數或存取憑證。不要分享含私人 token 的來源網址。
此商業預覽版停用內嵌官方網頁、官方短劇及官方服務入口，不會透過這些已停用功能建立網頁 cookies 或網頁儲存。選擇公開來源搜尋時，應用會先顯示搜尋字詞，再由你確認開啟外部瀏覽器；搜尋關鍵字會傳送至搜尋網站。外部瀏覽器及服務依其自身政策處理資料。

廣告、AI 與付款
此版本未啟用廣告或分析 SDK、發行者的雲端 AI、付費購買或權益驗證後端。你選取的影片來源或外部網站可能帶有該供應商的廣告。會員畫面不代表現在能付款。若未來啟用帳戶、付款、雲端 AI 或廣告服務，會先更新政策及相應披露。

保存、刪除與匯出
本機資料保留至你刪除相關項目、在 Android 設定清除本應用儲存空間或解除安裝。清除儲存空間會同時移除本機設定。你自行匯出或分享的 M3U 檔案可包含來源網址；應用不會刪除你另存於其他位置的副本。第三方服務的紀錄須按其政策向該服務要求刪除。

客服與安全
如你主動寄信，郵件服務及我們會處理你提供的地址及內容以回覆問題；請不要寄送密碼、支付卡資料或來源 token。客服信件只在處理問題及相關必要紀錄期間保留；可透過上述地址提出查詢或刪除要求。商業版透過 HTTPS 存取片單及媒體，不會略過憑證驗證。本政策不保證第三方服務的保存或安全措施。

Local data
Playlists and source URLs, subscriptions, favourites, recent channel choices, nickname, region preferences and line-check results stay on the device. This build has no publisher-operated cloud account or sync service. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled by app configuration.

Network requests and third parties
Importing/refreshing a playlist, loading programme data, playing or checking a stream sends requests to your chosen providers and their redirect/media servers. They can receive your IP address, request time, device/player request information and parameters or credentials contained in the URL. Do not share source URLs containing private tokens.
Embedded official webpages, official drama playback and official-service entry points are disabled in this commercial preview. These disabled features do not create web cookies or web storage. Public-source search displays the search words and asks you to confirm opening an external browser; those words are sent to the search website. External browsers and services process information under their own policies.

Advertising, AI and payments
Publisher advertising/analytics SDKs, cloud AI, purchases and the entitlement backend are not enabled in this build. External websites and streams may contain their providers’ advertisements. A membership screen does not mean purchases are available. This policy and relevant disclosures will be updated before account, billing, cloud-AI or advertising activation.

Retention and deletion
Local information remains until you delete the relevant item, clear this app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall. Clearing app storage also removes its settings. Exported/shared M3U files may contain source URLs; copies saved elsewhere are not deleted by the app. Contact third-party providers regarding their retained records.

Support and security
If you email support, our email provider and we process the address and message you send to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card data or source tokens. Support messages are retained only for resolving the request and necessary related records; contact us to ask about or request deletion. Commercial playlist/media requests use HTTPS without bypassing certificate validation. We cannot guarantee third-party retention or security practices.
