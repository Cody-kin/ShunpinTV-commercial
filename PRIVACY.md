# Privacy draft — not a final store disclosure

This preview stores channel lists, favorites, playback observations and repair reports on the device. Importing or playing a URL sends requests to that URL's servers. There is no advertising or analytics SDK enabled. Exported M3U files can contain source URLs; app-local exports are deleted on uninstall.

The publisher must add a support contact and publish a complete policy before launch. Any future ad SDK, identifiers, consent mechanisms or server-side logging require an updated policy and accurate Google Play Data safety declaration.
